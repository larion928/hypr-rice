## neoHtop

`home/.local/bin/neohtop` is built from [neoHtop](https://github.com/Abdenasser/neohtop) with one
change: the window is opaque (`"transparent": false` in `tauri.conf.json`), which works around a
WebKitGTK repaint bug on Wayland.
